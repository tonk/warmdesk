After the Norm overview in v0.52.0, the *Travel* sub-tab of the time-tracking *Overview* gets its own PDF export.

== Added

=== Travel overview as PDF

The *PDF* button next to *Settings* is now also shown on the *Travel* sub-tab. It downloads the overview of the selected year as a landscape A4 PDF, in the language of the interface and with your company logo. It contains the same four tables as the screen:

* travel per direction (outbound, return, no direction), with a totals row;
* travel per month, with a totals row;
* travel per customer;
* travel per route.

Every table shows the number of entries and days, the total time, your own time, the declarable time and the kilometres. When travel entries without a distance exist, the PDF starts with the same warning as the screen.

Long tables — a year with many routes, for example — continue on the next page with the table header repeated, so every page stays readable on its own. Administrators and time-tracking viewers get the PDF of the employee they are looking at.

== Upgrade

Nothing to migrate. There is one new endpoint, `GET /api/v1/time-entries/overview/travel/pdf`.
