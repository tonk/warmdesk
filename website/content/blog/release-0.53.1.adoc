A small fix for an annoying problem: losing what you typed in a dialog.

== Fixed

=== Dialogs no longer close after selecting text

When you selected text in a field of a dialog — the description of a time entry, for example — and released the mouse button just outside the dialog, the dialog closed and your input was gone. The browser reports such a press-and-release as a click on the grey backdrop, and that click closed the dialog.

A dialog now remembers where the mouse button was pressed. It only closes when both the press and the release happen on the backdrop. A normal click on the backdrop still closes it, and so do Escape and the ✕ button.

The fix applies to every dialog: time entries and the other standard dialogs, confirmations, About, the accessibility status, the news welcome, creating an invoice and managing projects in time tracking, and the invite picker in a group call.
