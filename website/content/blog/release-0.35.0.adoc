Until now the calendar view could copy one entry at a time. If this Thursday looks just like last Monday, that meant copying and pasting each block separately. `v0.35.0` lets you take the whole day along at once.

== Selecting

* *Ctrl+click* a block (*⌘* on macOS, *Shift* works too) to add it to the selection, or remove it. From the keyboard, tab to a block and press *Ctrl+Space*.
* Click a *day header* to select all entries of that day; *Ctrl+click* a header to add a day to what's already selected.

A bar above the calendar shows how many entries are selected, with *Copy*, *Delete*, and *Clear selection*. *Ctrl+C* copies the selection and *Esc* clears it. Right-clicking a selected block gives the same choices for the whole selection, and deleting asks for confirmation just once.

== Pasting

Right-click an empty slot and there are two choices:

* *Paste* — the earliest copied entry starts at the time you clicked, and the rest keep their distance to it. Copy Monday 09:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00, paste at 10:00 on Thursday, and you get 10:00–13:00 and 14:00–18:00.
* *Paste at original times* — only the date changes, so the day lands on its new date with the same times. This one is also on the right-click menu of a day header.

The copied entries stay available when you switch weeks, so copying a day into next week is: select the day, *Ctrl+C*, go to next week, right-click the day header, *Paste at original times*.

== Fixed

* With the app zoomed in or out, the calendar's right-click menu opened further away from the mouse the lower you clicked, and could end up out of view. It now opens where you click and stays inside the window.
* *Esc* closes the right-click menu wherever the keyboard focus is.
* The *Customer*, *Project*, and *Location* dropdowns in the calendar entry form are sorted alphabetically.
