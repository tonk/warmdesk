A small follow-up to v0.50.0: the *Travel* sub-tab of the time-tracking *Overview* now counts kilometres the same way as the *Norm* sub-tab.

== Changed

=== Kilometres of all entries

Until now the *Travel* sub-tab only counted the distance of entries on travel projects. If you record kilometres on a work entry instead — for example by picking a customer location on a *Lesgeven* row — those kilometres were missing there, while the *Norm* sub-tab did count them.

The *Travel* sub-tab now includes every entry with a distance. A work entry adds only its kilometres: to the month, the total, the customer, the direction (usually _No direction_, since it has no `- Heen` or `- Terug`) and the route, which is its description. Its time, entry count and day are not counted as travel time. Holiday entries are ignored.

The *travel without distance* warning still only looks at travel entries.

== Upgrade

Nothing to migrate. The travel API (`GET /api/v1/time-entries/overview/travel`) keeps its shape; the `distance` figures now include the kilometres of non-travel entries.
