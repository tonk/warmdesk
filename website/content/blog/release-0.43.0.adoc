This release adds a third way to look at the dashboard: next to *Tiles* and *List* there is now *My cards*.

== My cards

*My cards* lists every open card assigned to you, across all the projects you are a member of — whether you are the card's main assignee or one of its extra assignees. Closed cards, and cards in closed projects, are left out.

The table shows each card's key, title, project, column, priority, and due date. Click the key or title to open the card on its board, or the project name to open the board itself. The customer filter at the top of the dashboard works here too.

== Sortable columns

By default the cards are sorted by due date, soonest first. Overdue dates are shown in red, and cards without a due date are listed last. Click any column header to sort by it, and click again to reverse the order; sorting by priority puts the most urgent cards first. Your choice of view and sort order is remembered in your browser.

See the link:../../docs/user-guide/[User Guide] for details.
