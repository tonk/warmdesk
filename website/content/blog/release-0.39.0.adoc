`v0.38.0` added a lot to the Admin Panel: SLA calendars, a service catalogue, change management, CMDB types. With seventeen tabs in a row, the tab bar ran off the page. This release tidies that up and adds two smaller things people asked for.

== A grouped admin menu

The row of tabs is gone. The Admin Panel now has a menu on the left, grouped into *People & access*, *Customers & billing*, *Work*, *Helpdesk*, *Change & configuration*, and *System*. The former *Helpdesk* tab is now called *Ticket settings*, in the Helpdesk group alongside SLA policies, business hours, macros, checklists, and the service catalogue.

Every section has its own address, such as `/admin/helpdesk/sla-policies`. You can bookmark it or send it to a colleague, the browser's back button works, and the breadcrumb shows where you are. Fold away the groups you don't use by clicking their heading. On a phone the menu becomes a single dropdown.

Need more room for the menu, or for the page? Drag the divider between the two. You can also focus it and use the arrow keys, and double-click resets it. The width is remembered in your browser.

== Holidays for business hours

SLA deadlines can count in business hours, which should skip public holidays. Business-hours calendars now have the same *Add holidays* country menu as the time-tracking week view: pick a year and a country, and its public holidays are added to the calendar. Dates that are already on the list are skipped, and nothing is stored until you click *Save*, so it also works while you're still setting up a new calendar.

== Entries per group

Each group header in the time-tracking report now shows how many entries are in the group, next to its total:

----
Week 41 (Oct 5–Oct 11)        Entries: 33        45:30
----

The PDF and Excel exports show the same count, in the export's language.

The demo data (`warmdesk-seed`) also gained three business-hours calendars, two with Dutch public holidays.
