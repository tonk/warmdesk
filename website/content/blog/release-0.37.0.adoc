`v0.36.0` added a table under the time-report chart listing every activity. Handy, but with a lot of small activities the ones in *Other* made it a long list, and you had to add them up yourself to see how much *Other* actually was.

== One row for Other

The activities that make up the *Other* slice or bar are now combined into a single *Other* row, with their total time and their share of the total. Click the arrow on the left of that row to list them one by one, indented underneath; click it again to fold them back.

When you drill into *Other* on the chart, the row follows along: it only covers what's still left in *Other* at that level, and it folds back up whenever you change the report, chart type, time basis, or level.

See the Reports section of the link:../../docs/user-guide/[User Guide] for more.
