v0.10.25 retires the separate Reports screen and brings the project-board time report into Time Tracking as a first-class tab. Two new PDF export toggles and a tidier filter bar complete the release.

== Board report now lives in Time Tracking

The project-board time report has moved from its own menu entry into **Time Tracking → Board**.

Time Tracking now has three tabs:

* **Log Time** — the weekly timesheet (unchanged, requires the time-tracking permission).
* **Report** — personal time entry history with filters and export (unchanged, requires the time-tracking permission).
* **Board** — the project-board cross-user time report with PDF and XLSX export (requires the reports permission).

The old **Reports** menu entry has been removed. Any saved link or bookmark to `/reports` redirects automatically to the Board tab. Users who only have the reports permission (no personal time tracking) land directly on the Board tab when they open Time Tracking.

== PDF export options

Two new toggles in the **Export options** panel let you control what appears in the PDF:

* **Show page numbers** — include or omit the page-number footer on every page. On by default.
* **Show undeclarable time** — include or omit the undeclarable and declarable subtotal rows. On by default.

Both settings persist in the browser so they survive page reloads.

== Cleaner filter bar

The PDF font and language selectors have moved from the main filter bar into the **Export options** panel. The filter bar now fits comfortably on a single line at 1280 × 720 and above without wrapping.
